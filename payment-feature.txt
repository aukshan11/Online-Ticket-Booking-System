PAYMENT MODULE - VERSION 2.1

Feature: Online Payment Processing

1. Users can pay for their ticket bookings online.
2. The system sends payment details to the payment gateway.
3. The gateway returns a success or failure status.
4. On successful payment, the system confirms the booking.
5. On failed payment, the system displays an error message.

Purpose:
This feature improves the Online Ticket Booking System by documenting the payment workflow.
